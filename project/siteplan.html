Taste of Nigeria

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Taste of Nigeria

I chose the name "Taste of Nigeria" because the website will introduce visitors to popular Nigerian foods and traditional cuisine. The name connects directly to the main purpose of the website, which is to explore the different tastes, dishes, ingredients, and food traditions found in Nigeria.

2. Site Purpose

The website will provide information about popular Nigerian foods and traditional cuisine. It will include descriptions and pictures of different dishes such as Jollof Rice, Pounded Yam and Egusi Soup, Suya, Moi Moi, and Akara.

The website will also provide information about ingredients commonly used in Nigerian cooking and the regions where some foods are popular. The goal is to create a small but informative website that introduces visitors to Nigerian food and culture.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent information that visitors to the website may want to find.

  • What are some popular traditional Nigerian dishes and what ingredients are used in them?
  • Which Nigerian foods are popular in different regions of the country?
  • What does a traditional Nigerian dish such as Jollof Rice or Egusi Soup look like?

5. Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for major headings, the website logo, and section titles. Its bold appearance will help important information stand out.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs and general body content. It is easy to read and will work well on both mobile and desktop screens.

Reason for Choosing the Subject

I chose Nigerian food and cuisine because Nigerian food is an important part of the country's culture and is something I am interested in. It also provides enough content to create a small but informative website while giving me opportunities to practice HTML, CSS, responsive design, and JavaScript.
