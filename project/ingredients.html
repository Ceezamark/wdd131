Foods by region

Nigeria has hundreds of food traditions, so this is only a starting point. These four regions each have signature dishes.

North

Home to Hausa and Fulani cooking, where grains such as millet, sorghum, and rice are common and grilling and drying meat is a long tradition.

  • Suya, grilled meat with yaji spice
  • Tuwo shinkafa, a soft rice pudding eaten with soup
  • Kilishi, spiced dried meat
  • Masa, fried rice cakes

Southwest

The Yoruba homeland, known for palm oil, peppers, and many bean dishes.

  • Jollof rice, a party favorite
  • Pounded yam and egusi soup
  • Moi moi and akara, both made from black-eyed peas
  • Amala with ewedu and gbegiri soups

Southeast

The Igbo homeland, where yam is celebrated each year at the new yam festival.

  • Ofe onugbu, bitterleaf soup
  • Oha soup, thickened with cocoyam
  • Abacha, a cassava salad
  • Nkwobi, spiced cow foot

South-South

The Niger Delta region, where rivers and coastline make fish and seafood common.

  • Banga soup, made from palm fruit
  • Edikang ikong, a leafy vegetable soup
  • Afang soup, made with afang leaves
  • Pepper soup, a spicy broth
